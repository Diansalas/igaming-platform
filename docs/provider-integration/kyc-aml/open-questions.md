# KYC / AML package: minimum decisions and inputs

Each item: the question, why it is needed, and `identity-compliance`'s recommendation. The owner decides (compliance for
legal values). Items are ordered by when they block (OQ-KYC-01/02 first; the rest at the gate named). Nothing here is
decided by this package. No legal, regulatory or threshold value is proposed: those are compliance inputs.

| Id | Needed by | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-KYC-01 | K1 | Which KYC / screening / geolocation vendors are to be evaluated, and for which types? May one vendor cover KYC and screening (which is the only way the pseudonymous screening contract works without attributes)? | Evaluate 2 or 3 KYC document-verification vendors, preferring ones that can also screen the subject they verified (avoids sending attributes to a second party). Do not evaluate a geolocation vendor now (OQ-KYC-06). Owner, not an agent, handles vendor contact |
| OQ-KYC-02 | K0, K2 | Hosted/redirect collection only (vendor holds documents, platform holds status), or also platform-held document upload? | **Hosted/redirect only.** It keeps raw identity documents and PII out of our infrastructure (the stated posture), removes the need for the unbuilt encrypted document store, signed access URLs, malware scanning and retention tooling, and matches ADR 0028's forward note J12. It requires the additive hosted-session field (KYC-G01). Keep the existing upload code dormant, not deleted, until decided |
| OQ-KYC-03 | K0, K2 | May a vendor-derived verified residence country (and validity period) be recorded as market evidence, or does a staff reviewer keep making the residence determination (current behaviour)? | Staff determination stays the default for the first brand. Add vendor-supplied attributes (KYC-G02) only after the Q6 privacy review and an explicit owner decision, under the same evidence-collection-active check. The owner decision of 2026-10-16 names KYC-verified residence as the evidence source, not the vendor's unreviewed output |
| OQ-KYC-04 | K1, K8 | Commission the ADR 0116 Q6 privacy review: may name, date of birth and nationality be sent to a screening vendor, stored by us, or both? | Commission it now (documents only) so it is not on the critical path. Until it closes, screening works only on `VendorSubjectRef` from the same vendor that verified the subject. Do not store attributes |
| OQ-KYC-05 | K5 | Hold classes: should a **potential** sanctions match need four-eyes to lift (as a confirmed one does), given that it holds all value movement? | Yes before a real vendor (a distinct class with the same enforcement and lift rule; SR7-8). False positives will create four-eyes work; compliance owns the call and the staffing |
| OQ-KYC-06 | K0 | Geolocation: is a vendor wanted at all for the first brand, given the owner decision (market evidence = KYC-verified residence now, vendor geolocation later)? And how is registration evidenced, since it precedes KYC? | No geolocation vendor for the first brand. Registration/deposit/launch stay fail-closed without evidence; compliance decides what evidence is acceptable at registration (declared residence is client-supplied and not trusted by the gate). Revisit when a market requires per-request location |
| OQ-KYC-07 | K5, K8 | Compliance inputs for policy authoring: KYC trigger thresholds per licensing jurisdiction (cumulative deposit, play, EDD, tiers), screening lists, re-screen interval, backfill grace, effective-from, legal review reference | Compliance supplies them per jurisdiction; engineering authors the rows through the existing admin paths with four-eyes. No default, no seed, no invented value. Until supplied, KYC play/deposit triggers stay dormant (`not_required`) and screening is "unconfigured", which readiness shows as a failing item |
| OQ-KYC-08 | K8 | Data region(s), retention periods and erasure rules per jurisdiction; DPA and sub-processor acceptance | Owner and legal supply them per vendor; the vendor region must be selectable per tenant/jurisdiction (non-secret per-tenant configuration, PSP G-08). Retention stays legal configuration; nothing is purged by default |
| OQ-KYC-09 | K2 | Credential domains for screening and geolocation (X-G01): extend `providercred` domains (migration 0096 CHECK + ADR 0093 amendment) or give these vendors a platform-scope credential path? | Extend the domains (`aml_screening`, `geolocation`); one mechanism for all providers keeps rotation, revocation and four-eyes activation uniform. `architect` + `security` |
| OQ-KYC-10 | K2 | Post-approval vendor changes (KYC-G03): must the platform honour a vendor revocation/expiry of an approved verification? | Yes: record it as a governed, audited transition and fail enforcement closed. If the chosen vendor sends none (U-KYC-07), record the no-op as an accepted residual |
| OQ-KYC-11 | K2 | Screening before real money: build A7 follow-ups F1 (triggers, system actor, worker) and F2 (gate predicate) before the first real screening vendor? | Yes. A real vendor without F1/F2 only produces officer-attested records that nothing consumes. Sequence: Q6 review, F1, F2, then vendor adapter. `AML-SCREEN-TRIGGERS-1`, `AML-SCREEN-GATES-1` |
| OQ-KYC-12 | K8 | S4 transaction monitoring and S5 SAR are `NOT IMPLEMENTED` (`R-AML-MONITORING` always fails). Are they required before the first brand's real-money launch? | Compliance decides per licence (Anjouan first). Software capability here is not a filing capability. If required, they are separate work items with their own gates; a SAR regulator format is a legal input |
| OQ-KYC-13 | K6 | One vendor workspace/account per tenant? | Yes for any tenant that goes live; the first synthetic tenant needs one. Required for callback tenant binding |

## Inputs (not decisions) the owner must eventually supply

Listed in `integration-gates.md` ("Exact inputs needed"): vendor documentation, sandbox terms and credentials via the secret
store, synthetic test subjects and documents, the privacy-review outcome, compliance's policy values, DPA / region /
retention, written vendor answers to the open documentation items, and later (K9 only) the signed contract and
production credentials. None is requested now.
