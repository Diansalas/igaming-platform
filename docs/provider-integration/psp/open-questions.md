# Fiat PSP package: minimum decisions and inputs

Each item: the question, why it is needed, and `payments`' recommendation. The owner decides. Items are ordered by when
they block (OQ-1/2 now; the rest at the gate named). Nothing here is decided by this package.

| Id | Needed by | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-1 | G1 | Is Nuvei the provider to evaluate, and may 1 to 2 alternative fiat PSPs be put through the same checklist in parallel before a commercial relationship is committed? Commercial/market fit (EU member states, LATAM countries, Anjouan-licensed iGaming merchant acceptance, fees) cannot be decided from documentation. | Yes to both. Keep Nuvei as candidate A, run the identical `nuvei-evaluation.md` checklist on one or two others (the file is written provider-agnostically), and pick on documented fit plus commercial terms. The owner (not an agent) handles vendor contact. |
| OQ-2 | G1, G2 | (a) Allow the documentation host (`docs.nuvei.com`) in the documentation-review session, or nominate a person to supply the pages. (b) After G1, authorize the adapter build against **recorded fixtures only** (no sandbox call). | (a) Yes: read-only public docs, no account, no credential. (b) Yes, conditional on a G1 go; keeps the vendor dependency at fixtures until the sandbox gate. |
| OQ-3 | G0/G2 | If the chosen PSP's notification signature has **no signed timestamp** (G-02) or the signature is not in a header (G-01): reject that PSP, or authorize an ADR 0022 amendment (replay defence by idempotency-only for a named scheme with compensating controls, and/or a bounded body view for `Extract`)? | Prefer another PSP or vendor-side configuration if the choice is free (the amendment weakens a deliberate platform rule, ADR 0022 s3 pt 10). | OQ-4 | G2 | First-adapter scope: deposits only, or deposits + payouts? | **Deposits only** (Option 1 of the 2026-10-10 sandbox request). Add payouts as a second adapter increment. |
| OQ-5 | G5 | For card flows with authorise-then-capture: which vendor status makes the deposit `Succeeded` (credit the player)? | OQ-7 | G9 (payout scope) | Governed exit for a refused payout park (PAY-PAYOUT-CONTRADICTION-HOLD-1), M4 non-MOCK evidence standard (D-7) and WD050/WD051 backstop for real payouts. | Out of scope while deposits-only. Decide before the payout increment; do not enable non-MOCK M4. |
