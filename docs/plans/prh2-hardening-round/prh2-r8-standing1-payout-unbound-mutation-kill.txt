CORRECTION of the X6 rationale given in the 5467063 table above (LF C1). That rationale ("both
references belong to P1, neither is attributable to B") considered only the line resolved by l.ref as
P1's settlement reference. It missed the l.settlement shape: matchPayment resolves the line through
bySettlement[l.settlement] to P1, and l.ref is an UNRELATED PSP reference R. Under the negative
attribution in force at 5467063, an UNLINKED withdrawal_completed keyed by R cleared B under the
original (on l.ref = R) but not under X6 (on a.providerRef = P1's reference). So X6 WAS
distinguishable at 5467063; calling it "equivalent" there was wrong, and no test built that shape.
Under the positive rule it is equivalent:
  - B clears on R only if R is B's OWN release (bySettlement[R] == B). matchPayment looks up
    byRef[payout R], then bySettlement[l.ref], before bySettlement[l.settlement] and the merchant
    fallback, so such a line resolves to B itself and never reaches the cross-check B step.
  - If another attempt holds R as its provider reference, the line resolves to that attempt, and the
    byRef check refuses clearing on R and on a.providerRef alike.
  - In the l.ref = settlement shape (TestD2_9b), both references belong to P1 and neither is B's own
    release.
