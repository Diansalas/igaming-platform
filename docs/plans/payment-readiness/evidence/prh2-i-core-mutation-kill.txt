  1. applied by hand via a Python one-shot `str.replace` on an exact,

  2. built (`go build`/`go vet -tags integration`) to confirm the mutant

    File: internal/alerting/guarded.go, RaiseGuarded.
    Mutation: replaced the `tx.Begin(ctx)` (SAVEPOINT)/Commit/Rollback-to-

    no isolation boundary.
    Killed by: TestRaiseGuarded_PersistentSwallowedFailure_FallsBackToRaiseFailed
        (go test -tags=integration -run
         TestRaiseGuarded_PersistentSwallowedFailure_FallsBackToRaiseFailed

Not attempted in this pass (disclosed, not silently dropped - each would
need either a fault-injection hook this ADR does not specify, or a real
I-wire business call site that does not exist yet):
  - "The savepoint widened to cover a business statement (Q3)" - would
    require mutating a real business call site alongside RaiseGuarded;
    no such call site exists in I-core.
  - "An incrementing attempt_no for unrouted" - covered structurally by
    the `CHECK (event <> 'unrouted' OR attempt_no = 0)` constraint itself
    (a mutant Go dispatcher that tried to increment it would simply fail
    the INSERT, exercised implicitly by
    TestDispatcher_ZeroRoutesProducesUnroutedAndMetaOccurrence's
    idempotency-across-3-passes assertion, but not isolated as its own
    named mutation run here).
  - "A `run:<uuid>` discriminator" - no reconciliation call site exists in
    I-core to mutate; the stable-discriminator convention is enforced only
    by the I-wire call sites' own code, not by this package.
  - "The attribute trigger deleted" (the general allowlist, as distinct
    from the raise_failed-specific one in M6) - reasoned as equivalent in
    mechanism to M6 and M9; not independently re-run for time.
  - "The subject-read policy without its exclusions" - reasoned
    equivalent in mechanism to M8 (same C-102-9 exclusion-set pattern
    repeated per family); not independently re-run for time.
  - "`RaiseDetached` choosing its scope from the Alert" - structurally
    impossible rather than merely guarded: `Alert` has no scope-bearing
    field for `RaiseDetached` to read in the first place (see scope.go's
    own doc comment), so there is no single-line mutation that reproduces
    this defect without a larger redesign: the ADR-listed kill test
    (TestRLS_CrossTenantSubjectRaiseRefused) is exercised as a positive
    test in the normal suite instead.
