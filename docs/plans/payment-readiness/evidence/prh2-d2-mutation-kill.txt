Notes
-----
- A first battery at 32dc4f0 (where the unbound rule also required `byMerchant`) had one surviving
  mutant, "byMerchant dropped", equivalent by construction (an unbound park holds no reference, so
  a line can only resolve to it by merchant reference). The rule was changed at 9ff8757 to not gate
  on how the line resolved (more conservative: an unbound reason on a reference-holding attempt stays
  loud) and pinned by test; the re-run above has no survivor.
- "An M1 or investigation_status change does not clear it" (LF (c) 3): investigation_status is
  exercised through ResolveMismatch (TestD2_3 .../investigation_status_resolved_does_not_clear).
  M1 is NOT IMPLEMENTED (ADR 0101, K3); the emission predicate reads no resolution or prior finding.
- The D1 poll reasons are inserted by fixture (ApplyDisputeFromNonTerminal on a real pending
  deposit, with the plan's reason strings); D1's code is not used.
